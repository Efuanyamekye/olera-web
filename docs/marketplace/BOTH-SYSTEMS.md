## The one line

**PG2 is the join.** A provider who buys the staffing add-on becomes, on the MedJobs side, an active
client with a staffing need — the left-hand input to match and fulfilment. Everything past that point
is MedJobs' machine: ST8 supplies the candidate, QUAL vets them, MA1 through MA5 carry the hire to a
bill.

Read the other way, the marketplace is MedJobs' demand side and MedJobs is the supply side of B3.
That is the whole relationship, which is why the line is single: one handoff, one object, no shared
state on either side of it.

## What does not cross

A family never appears in MedJobs. A student never appears in the marketplace. The only object that
crosses is a provider with a staffing need — and it crosses once, in one direction.

## The open question the line creates

Both sides have a billing event. PG2 is a subscription the provider buys in the portal; MA5 issues a
bill after six shifts are worked. A provider who buys PG2 and then hires through MA1–MA5 would meet
both. Charging twice for one hire is the obvious risk, and charging nothing twice is the obvious
overcorrection. Neither system decides this today, because neither knows about the other.
