# Operating map

One figure covering both sides of the business: how care seekers, care providers,
employers and care workers enter, what happens to them once they are active, and
what each one gets out of it.

`operating-map.html` is the source. It is the body of a published artifact — the
`<!doctype>`, `<html>` and `<head>` wrapper is added at publish time — so it also
opens directly in a browser. Every connector is drawn at runtime from the measured
positions of the cards, which is why nothing needs pixel alignment by hand.

## Shape

**Top funnel** — four lanes of acquisition. Care seeker and care provider under
*One city*; employer and care worker under *University site*.

**Consumer relations management** — one meeting covering six things, sitting across
the top of the bottom funnel because it touches everything below it.

**Bottom funnel** — three tracks fed by one line out of consumer relations
management: TA aid establishment, TB care establishment, TC care worker hiring.

**Success** — what each party ends up with, and where Olera charges.

## Provisional codes

**CW1** and **CO** are new. Neither had a code in the earlier maps, so both are

## Not yet true

The map is a target, not a description of what ships today. Anything in it that
does not exist yet — TC as a tracked state, the paid products as sold products,
consumer relations management as one meeting — needs checking against the code
before it is quoted as current.
